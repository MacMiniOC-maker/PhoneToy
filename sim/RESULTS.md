# Simulation results

Generated by `python3 sim/run.py` with ngspice. These are models, not measurements: they tell you what to expect and what to check with a multimeter.

Switching at the firmware's 1 kHz dimming rate: on in 27 ns, off in 433 ns, against a 1,000,000 ns period.

## 3. USB power budget (arithmetic, not simulation)

Typical datasheet figures and estimates. The LED figure is the 150 Ω row above, the brightest plausible case.

| Load | Typical | Peak |
|---|---|---|
| ESP32-S3 with Wi-Fi | 100 mA | 340 mA |
| Four button lights | 51 mA | 51 mA |
| Ringer amplifier while ringing (about 0.25 W into 4 Ω) | 55 mA | 55 mA |
| Earpiece amplifier, microphone, idle draw | 8 mA | 8 mA |
| **Total** | **214 mA** | **454 mA** |

A 5 V 2 A charger has plenty of margin. A computer USB port (500 mA) is fine for bench work, with little to spare on Wi-Fi peaks (454 mA).
